# Evidence Log — Garden City Facility (Marathon Digital / MARA Holdings)

County: Glasscock · Announced: 200 MW · Sponsor: Marathon Digital Holdings (MARA, SEC-reporting), acquired from Applied Digital (APLD, SEC-reporting)

## Site control
**Evidence found — strong.** Definitive agreement announced March 15, 2024; purchase price $87.3M net / $97.3M gross. Disclosed via both companies' own SEC-adjacent investor-relations press releases — a public-to-public transaction, strongly auditable.
- Sources: [MARA IR](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1347/marathon-digital-holdings-enters-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-200-megawatt-bitcoin-mining-data-center-adjacent-to-a-wind-farm), [Applied Digital IR](https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/89/applied-digital-announces-agreement-to-sell-garden-city) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Physical commitment
**No public evidence found (as of 2026-08-05).** No specific TCEQ docket found (dead end); the facility has operated since 2023 under the prior owner so presumably already holds relevant permits, but they weren't located this session.

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — partial.** Facility is powered by stranded/flared natural gas from adjacent oil wells — an unusual and well-documented power arrangement, though not itself a dollar-denominated financial commitment. No specific interconnection fee found.

## Incentive filings
**No public evidence found (as of 2026-08-05).** Not found on the JETI current-agreements list. Explicitly **not** among the matches surfaced on the Comptroller's Qualifying Data Center registry either (Marathon Digital was named in the "not found" category of that check) — a real dead end, not an oversight.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — mixed, both sides documented.** MARA (public company) has a documented history of SEC accounting restatements (full-year and quarterly results for 2021 and Q1–Q3 2022) following an SEC comment letter, and cancelled/postponed a Q4 2022 earnings call — a real negative corporate-track-record signal. Separately, Garden City itself has operated without major reported incident since 2023 (first under Applied Digital, now under Marathon) — a positive site-level operating record. Both facts are logged; they point in different directions and shouldn't be averaged away.
