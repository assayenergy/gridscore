# Evidence Log — Abilene Campus (Stargate + Microsoft AI Factory)

County: Taylor · Announced: 2,100 MW · Sponsor: Crusoe Energy (operator) / Lancium (landowner)

## Site control
**Evidence found — partial/inferred.** Company-disclosed partnership structure: "Lancium owns the ground... Crusoe designs, builds and operates" (same structure as Childress, see #11). No specific acreage/deed figure found for the Abilene parcel itself (dead end on that specific number). Indirect confirmation: local budget reporting states the campus represents up to 32% of Abilene's and 25% of Taylor County's FY2025 budgeted property tax revenue — implying formal county tax-roll registration, though the underlying record wasn't pulled directly.
- Sources: [Crusoe/Microsoft release](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-announces-new-900-mw-ai-factory-campus-in-abilene-texas-to-support-microsoft-ai-infrastructure) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Physical commitment
**Evidence found — strong.** TCEQ Standard Permit for Crusoe's "Longhorn Data Center" approved Jan 22, 2025, authorizing ~360 MW behind-the-meter natural gas generation across ten simple-cycle turbines, explicitly limited to "onsite use only for data centers and computing." A further TCEQ NSR case-by-case permit application was filed Nov 7, 2025, for additional behind-the-meter generation tied to the 2.1GW total target.
- Sources: [MLQ Infrastructure permit filing](https://mlq.ai/permit-filings/usa/texas/abilene/182126/), [TCEQ technical review](https://records.tceq.texas.gov/cs/idcplg?IdcService=TCEQ_EXTERNAL_SEARCH_GET_FILE&dID=8600163&Rendition=Web) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — partial.** Microsoft's 900MW lease and OpenAI's 1.2GW Stargate phase are both publicly confirmed by the companies themselves. Crusoe separately disclosed a $3.4B joint venture with Blue Owl Capital for AI data center development (financing vehicle, not site-specific to Abilene alone). No specific ERCOT interconnection fee or agreement dollar figure was found (dead end).

## Incentive filings
**Evidence found.** Lancium appears on the Texas Comptroller's Qualifying Data Center registry (Tax Code §151.359 sales-tax exemption program) under multiple "Abilene Clean Campus" entries (I–VIII). This is a distinct program from JETI/Chapter 403 — it confirms state registration, not a school-district tax abatement. Not found on the JETI current-agreements list (checked directly, zero match).
- Source: [Comptroller Data Center lists](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/data-centers/data-center-lists.php) — captured 2026-08-05. **Caveat:** this finding came from an AI-summarized fetch of the registry page, not a manual read of the raw table — recommend confirming directly before citing in the final publication.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — positive.** Crusoe delivered Abilene Phase 1 (two buildings, 980,000 sq ft, 200+ MW) from groundbreaking (June 2024) to fully operational running OCI/Nvidia workloads (Sept 2025) — roughly 15 months, a genuinely fast, on-record delivery. Lancium has now used the same land-owner/interconnect-partner structure on two sites (Abilene + Childress, see #11), both ERCOT-approved.
- Sources: [Lanin Substack, delivery timeline](https://lanin.substack.com/p/crusoe-is-not-a-neocloud-its-americas), [Data Center Frontier](https://www.datacenterfrontier.com/hyperscale/article/55276169/crusoe-adds-45-gw-natural-gas-to-fuel-ai-expands-abilene-data-center-to-12-gw) — captured 2026-08-05.
