# Evidence Log — Kaufman County Campus (Prometheus Hyperscale)

County: Kaufman · Announced: 200 MW (4×50MW) · Sponsor: Prometheus Hyperscale

## Site control
**Evidence found — partial/self-reported.** ~500-acre site west of Dallas, between Talty and Post Oak Bend City. No independent CAD confirmation found (dead end).

## Physical commitment
**No public evidence found (as of 2026-08-05).** Jenbacher J620 natural gas engines and an Engie-operated battery storage system are disclosed as the planned power source, but no TCEQ air-permit docket was found (dead end).

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — partial.** Named commercial partnerships with ENGIE and Conduit Power for behind-the-meter power generation and storage. No specific dollar figure or interconnection fee found for the Kaufman County site itself.

## Incentive filings
**No public evidence found (as of 2026-08-05).** Not found on the JETI current-agreements list or the Comptroller's Qualifying Data Center registry — dead end on both.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — mixed.** Founder Trenton Thornock has **no direct prior data-center company background** — his career prior to Prometheus was in industrial projects and energy-sector finance (zinc recycling facilities, drilling services CFO roles), by his own account. The company has backfilled data-center-specific expertise at the leadership level: Bernard Looney (former BP CEO) as Chairman/CEO, and James Faccone (20+ years, ex-Meta/Equinix infrastructure delivery) as Global Head of Infrastructure Delivery. Prometheus's other flagship project is in Wyoming (1.25GW) — this is a multi-state, still-unproven-at-scale company with no delivered facility yet found in either state.
