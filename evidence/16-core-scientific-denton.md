# Evidence Log — Denton Campus (Core Scientific)

County: Denton · Announced: 391 MW existing site + $1.2B CoreWeave expansion · Sponsor: Core Scientific, Inc. (CORZ, SEC-reporting)

## Site control
**Evidence found — strong, government-counterparty-confirmed.** Core Scientific leases 78.85 acres of city-owned land from the City of Denton (near the Denton Energy Center), atop its original 31-acre owned site (built 2022), expanding by 42.9 acres including 6.7 acres purchased for a solid-waste-transfer-station parcel. Denton City Council approved amendments to the power purchase agreements and land leases on Nov 19, 2024 — this is about as strong as site-control evidence gets short of a raw deed, since it's confirmed via a government body's own meeting action.
- Sources: [dentonrc.com](https://dentonrc.com/news/denton_municipal_electric/core-scientific-which-has-a-denton-ai-data-center-will-be-sold-to-n-j/article_d0b3318b-303e-4543-b3ad-55f3bfcb5e58.html), [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241120134811/en/Core-Scientific-Announces-Approval-of-Lease-Amendments-with-the-City-of-Denton-to-Enable-High-Performance-Computing-Expansion) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Incentive filings
**Evidence found — registry match (corrected 2026-09-28; see `corrections.md`).** The Comptroller's
"Registered Qualifying Large Data Center Projects" table lists, verbatim: "Denton Data Center |
09/19/2024 | [owner blank] | Coreweave Compute Acquisition Co V LLC (occupant, LD394824-OC2) | Core
Scientific, Inc. / Coreweave, Inc. (operators, LD394824-OP1 / LD394824-OP2)". Core Scientific is a
listed operator; the registry prints no city or county, so the tie to this campus rests on the
name plus Core Scientific's own 10-K listing of a "Denton, Texas" site (INFERENCE, a strong one).
No JETI agreement or county Ch. 312 abatement found. Score 7 stands (registry match, same tier as
the other registry-only projects).
- **What the 2026-08-05 note got wrong:** it read "Cottonwood, Cedarvale" as this project's
  entries. Neither is Denton. "Cottonwood Data Center" (eff. 09/12/2025; Core Scientific as owner,
  occupant and operator) is Core Scientific's Pecos, Texas site: the FY2024 10-K, p. 8, lists
  "Pecos, Texas ("Cottonwood")". "Cedarvale, Barslow/Pyote TX Data Center" is the Ward County site
  Core Scientific sold in the bankruptcy (10-K, Note 3, p. 96, the "Cedarvale Facility"); the
  registry now lists Ionic Digital and Nscale entities for it.
- **Statute (resolved 2026-09-28):** the Denton row sits under "Registered Qualifying Large Data
  Center Projects" (LD-prefix registrations). That table implements Tax Code §151.3595, "PROPERTY
  USED IN CERTAIN LARGE DATA CENTER PROJECTS; TEMPORARY EXEMPTION" (the Comptroller overview page
  lists "Tax Code Section 151.3595" under Laws). §151.3595(e): the owner, operator or occupant "shall
  apply to the comptroller for certification and for the issuance of a registration number."
  Certification under §151.3595(d) requires at least 40 qualifying jobs, a capital investment of at
  least $500 million over five years, and a contract for at least 20 MW of transmission capacity.
  Registry-based scores across this repository are now labeled by table: §151.3595 for LD entries,
  §151.359 for DC entries.
- Sources: [Comptroller data center lists](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/data-centers/data-center-lists.php)
  — read directly, captured 2026-09-28; [10-K FY2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839341/000162828025008302/core-20241231.htm),
  pp. 8, 96 — captured 2026-09-28.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — mixed. Score 10/20, unchanged; basis restated 2026-09-28 (see `corrections.md`).**
- **Cited:** Core Scientific filed for Chapter 11 on 2022-12-21 ("On December 21, 2022, the Company
  and certain of its affiliates … filed voluntary petitions … in the United States Bankruptcy Court
  for the Southern District of Texas", Note 1, p. 84) and emerged on 2024-01-23 ("On January 23,
  2024 … the Company emerged from bankruptcy", Item 1, p. 10). Source:
  [10-K FY2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839341/000162828025008302/core-20241231.htm),
  captured 2026-09-28.
- **Cited:** the Denton site has been an operating Core Scientific facility since 2022 (10-K FY2024,
  p. 8, power-provider table: "Denton Municipal Electric 391 Denton, Texas"; site built 2022 per the
  Site control sources above). That is site-level delivery.
- **UNVERIFIED:** the 2026-08-05 text said the site has operated "without a major reported incident"
  since 2022. No public source was found for that, and the record is not clean: the City of Denton
  and Denton Municipal Electric had claims against Core Scientific in the bankruptcy, settled by
  court order on 2023-08-16 for lease cure costs of $1.5 million (10-K FY2024, Note 3, "City of
  Denton Lease Settlement", p. 95). Searched 2026-09-28 for fires, outages and enforcement at the
  site; none found in web search, but city code-enforcement, fire and court databases were not
  searched. Absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.
- **Corrected 2026-09-28: no CoreWeave acquisition.** The 2026-08-05 text said Core Scientific
  "is being acquired by CoreWeave in a ~$9B deal." That was already stale when captured. Core
  Scientific's stockholders did not approve the merger, and "on October 30, 2025 … Core Scientific
  terminated the Merger Agreement, effective immediately" (8-K filed 2025-10-30, Item 1.02,
  [accession 0001140361-25-039808](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839341/000114036125039808/ef20057995_8k.htm),
  captured 2026-09-28). Core Scientific remains the independent sponsor; CoreWeave is a tenant and
  co-operator (see Incentive filings).
