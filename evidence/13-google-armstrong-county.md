# Evidence Log — Panhandle Campus, Armstrong County (Google)

County: Armstrong · Announced MW: not disclosed per-site (part of bundled $40B/6,200MW+ statewide PPA announcement) · Sponsor: Google

## Site control
**Evidence found.** 1,300 acres near Claude, TX; construction underway, contracted to Crusoe Energy as builder (reported underway since ~May 2026).
- Sources: [ABC7 Amarillo](https://abc7amarillo.com/news/local/google-announces-new-data-center-in-armstrong-county-texas-judge-adam-ensey-crusoe-energy-ercot-southwest-power-pool-claude-texas), [Amarillo Tribune, 2025-11-16](https://amarillotribune.org/2025/11/16/google-to-add-data-center-to-armstrong-county/) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Physical commitment
**Evidence found — strong, directly tied.** Crusoe Energy Systems filed a TCEQ air construction permit application (~Feb 5, 2026) for the "Goodnight Data Center Power Plant" — up to 933MW via 10 simple-cycle gas turbines (GE Vernova/Solar Turbines), explicitly built "to power a co-located AI data center being developed by Google." This is a direct, docket-level link between a named permit applicant and this specific Google site.
- Sources: [oilandgaswatch.org](https://oilandgaswatch.org/alert/rec_d64au9u349rifoo9o9u0), [theenergymag.com](https://theenergymag.com/news/2026-07-31/crusoe-texas-ai-data-center-goodnight) — captured 2026-08-05.
