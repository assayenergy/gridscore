# Evidence Log — Corsicana Facility (Riot Platforms)

County: Navarro · Announced: 1,000 MW · Sponsor: Riot Platforms, Inc. (RIOT, SEC-reporting)

## Site control
**Evidence found — strong.** Sequential, SEC-disclosed land purchases: 265 acres (~2022), +355 acres (May 2025), +238 acres (July 2025) = 858 acres total. Public-company disclosures create an auditable trail even without direct CAD confirmation.
- Sources: [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/04/18/2865456/0/en/Riot-Energizes-New-Corsicana-Facility-in-Navarro-County-Texas.html), [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/riot-platforms-acquires-additional-238-acres-at-corsicana-texas-campus/) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — strongest in the universe.** Riot states its Corsicana facility received "the first 1GW load ever approved by ERCOT" — a claim repeated across multiple independent trade-press outlets (not just Riot's own materials), making it the most independently corroborated ERCOT-approval claim of any project in this list.
- Sources: [Riot Platforms press release](https://www.riotplatforms.com/investors/news-events/press-releases/detail/139/riot-announces-groundbreaking-at-1-gw-data-center-facility), [SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1167419/000155837025000282/riot-20250121xex99d1.htm) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — positive, auditable.** Riot delivered the initial 400MW on schedule by April 2024, per its own SEC filings, and has since expanded the site on a documented, sequential basis. SEC-reporting status makes this one of the more independently auditable track records in the universe (deeper due-diligence on Riot's broader corporate/financial history was not performed this session).
