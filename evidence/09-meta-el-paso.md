# Evidence Log — El Paso AI Data Center (Meta)

County: El Paso · Announced: 1,000 MW · Sponsor: Meta Platforms (via SPV "Wurldwide LLC")

## Site control
**Evidence found — strong.** Meta used a wholly-formed SPV, "Wurldwide LLC," to purchase 1,039 acres of city-owned land in far northeast El Paso for $8.57M ($8,250/acre), sale approved by El Paso City Council Dec 4, 2023, deal closed Dec 29, 2023. Full evidence: named affiliate LLC, government counterparty (the city itself), council-minute-level confirmation.
- Sources: [cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/meta-el-paso-data-center-1gw/), [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/meta-behind-1000-acre-campus-proposal-in-el-paso-texas/) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Physical commitment
**Evidence found — strong.** El Paso Electric filed a PUC application for "McCloud Generation" — 366MW across 813 modular natural gas generators (450kW each) — to power the site under a 5-year exclusive "bridge" arrangement, expected online 2027, cost ~$473M "paid for by Meta." This is a PUC (not TCEQ) filing but functions as strong physical-commitment evidence given the direct generation build tied to this specific site.
- Sources: [El Paso Matters, 2026-01-18](https://elpasomatters.org/2026/01/18/el-paso-electric-meta-data-center-new-473-million-natural-gas-power-plant/), [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/meta-to-deploy-366mw-of-modular-gas-units-to-power-1gw-data-center-in-el-paso-texas/) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — strong.** Total investment raised from $1.5B to $10B (announced March 26, 2026). McCloud plant cost recovery structured as a PUC-approved rate charged to Meta specifically. Concrete, escalating, multiply-sourced financial disclosure.
- Source: [CNBC, 2026-03-26](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/26/meta-to-spend-10-billion-on-ai-data-center-in-el-paso-1gw-by-2028.html) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Incentive filings
**Evidence found — strong.** El Paso City Council approved an 80% city-tax abatement over 25 years as part of a 2023 economic development agreement (est. ~$400M in foregone city tax revenue over the term); El Paso County Commissioners Court approved a parallel county-level tax break the same year. Notably, in June 2026 the City Council voted to **reject** an effort to terminate the agreement — evidence the commitment is real, binding, and politically contested (not vaporware).
- Sources: [El Paso Matters, 2026-06-09](https://elpasomatters.org/2026/06/09/meta-data-center-deal-to-remain-el-paso-city-council-rejects-terminating-agreement/), [elpasoinc.com](https://www.elpasoinc.com/news/local_news/el-paso-city-council-votes-to-keep-meta-economic-incentive-deal-intact/article_2ee47d8e-e745-4418-8979-0610933a45be.html) — captured 2026-08-05. Not a JETI/Ch.403 filing specifically — this is a municipal/county Chapter 380/381-style abatement, which fits the spec's signal #4 category directly.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — strongest tier.** Meta is one of the world's largest hyperscale data-center operators with a decades-long, extensively documented delivery record across dozens of campuses globally. General knowledge plus company's own portfolio disclosure.
- Source: [Meta Data Centers portfolio](https://datacenters.atmeta.com/) — captured 2026-08-05.
