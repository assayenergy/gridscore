# Evidence Log — Data Center Technology Park (Tract)

County: Caldwell · Announced: 2,000+ MW · Sponsor: Tract (land/infrastructure developer — no named data-center operator/tenant yet)

## Site control
**Evidence found — strong.** Tract closed acquisition of 1,515 acres in Caldwell County (announced via GlobeNewswire, May 7, 2025), later expanded to ~3,000 acres via two additional acquisitions (Jan 2026). This is a corporate-disclosed closed transaction, not merely an option — strong for a land developer, though not independently CAD-verified this session (Caldwell CAD's own portal — esearch.caldwellcad.org — was identified but not queryable via available tools).
- Source: [GlobeNewswire, 2025-05-07](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/05/07/3076274/0/en/TRACT-CLOSES-ACQUISITION-OF-1-515-ACRES-IN-CALDWELL-COUNTY-TEXAS-FOR-MULTI-GIGAWATT-DATA-CENTER-TECHNOLOGY-PARK.html) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Physical commitment
**No public evidence found (as of 2026-08-05).** No air/building permits expected yet — Tract has not named a data-center operator/tenant for this site, consistent with its business model as a land/infrastructure developer rather than an operator (see Sponsor track record).

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — partial.** Tract executed a Facility Design Agreement with Blue Bonnet Electric Cooperative (a named utility counterparty) targeting energization of an initial 360MW by 2028. This is a concrete utility-side commitment, though well short of a full interconnection agreement for the site's full multi-gigawatt scale.

## Incentive filings
**No public evidence found (as of 2026-08-05).** Not found on the JETI current-agreements list or the Comptroller's Qualifying Data Center registry — dead end on both checks.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — mixed, at the founder level.** Tract's founder, Grant van Rooyen, has an extensive and well-documented prior track record: founded and led Cologix as CEO for 8 years (recapitalized at ~$1.4B in 2017, ~$4B in 2022), later acquired Teraco (South Africa) and sold 51% to Digital Realty for ~$3.5B. That said, **Tract the company** has not yet delivered an operating data center on this specific site — it remains at the land/infrastructure stage, with no named tenant. Strong individual pedigree; zero site-specific delivery yet.
- Source: [DCD profile of Grant van Rooyen](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/analysis/building-for-the-long-term-grant-van-rooyens-tract-masterplan/) — captured 2026-08-05.
