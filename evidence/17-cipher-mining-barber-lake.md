# Evidence Log — Barber Lake (Cipher Mining)

County: Mitchell · Announced: 300 MW · Sponsor: Cipher Mining Inc. (CIFR, SEC-reporting)

## Site control
**Evidence found — strong.** Acquisition closed September 2024 for $67.5M cash plus a $3/MWh variable fee for 5 years post-energization; 587-acre site. Disclosed via Cipher's own SEC-adjacent press releases (public company).
- Source: [SeekingAlpha/Cipher PR](https://seekingalpha.com/pr/19857490-cipher-mining-announces-the-closing-of-its-acquisition-of-barber-lake-300-mw-data-center-site) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Physical commitment
**Evidence found — partial.** Public TCEQ records reference a "Barber Lake Cipher Portable Plant" air-quality permit activity — appears tied to construction-support/temporary equipment rather than permanent backup generation. The specific permit number was not located (dead end). This counts as partial evidence of TCEQ engagement, not a confirmed full-scale generation permit.

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — strong.** 10-year HPC colocation agreement with Fluidstack: ~$830M contracted revenue, up to $9.0B potential lease value including extensions, plus a $333M Google backstop supporting project debt. Well-documented via SEC filings and specialist trade press.
- Source: [TheMinerMag, 2025-11-20](https://www.theminermag.com/news/2025-11-20/cipher-fluidstack-hpc) — captured 2026-08-05.

- Source: [Comptroller Data Center lists](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/data-centers/data-center-lists.php) — captured 2026-08-05. Manual-verification caveat applies as with other registry citations.
