# Evidence Log — Barber Lake (Cipher Mining)

County: Mitchell · Announced: 300 MW · Sponsor: Cipher Mining Inc. (CIFR, SEC-reporting)

## Site control
**Evidence found — strong.** Acquisition closed September 2024 for $67.5M cash plus a $3/MWh variable fee for 5 years post-energization; 587-acre site. Disclosed via Cipher's own SEC-adjacent press releases (public company).
- Source: [SeekingAlpha/Cipher PR](https://seekingalpha.com/pr/19857490-cipher-mining-announces-the-closing-of-its-acquisition-of-barber-lake-300-mw-data-center-site) — captured 2026-08-05.

registration covers emergency/standby diesel generators (60 × 3.0 MW and 60 × 3.2 MW, plus 30 × 3.0
