# Evidence Log — Project Matador (Fermi America)

County: Carson (primary; site footprint also reported touching Potter/Randall — unresolved, see below) · Announced: 11,000 MW · Sponsor: Fermi America (Fermi Inc., ticker FRMI)

## Site control
**Evidence found.** Fermi America holds a 99-year sovereign leasehold over ~5,855 acres in Carson County, granted by the Texas Tech University System — a named, public-entity counterparty, not an opaque LLC. Fermi controls access/development/improvement rights to all 5,855 acres.
- Source: [Amarillo Tribune, 2025-11-17](https://amarillotribune.org/2025/11/17/fermi-americas-lease-with-the-texas-tech-university-system-establishes-research-facilities-scholarship-fund-and-more/) — captured 2026-08-05.
- **Open question (not resolved):** multiple sources place the site across Carson/Potter/Randall county lines. [Amarillo Tribune, 2025-10-08](https://amarillotribune.org/2025/10/08/digging-into-fermis-filings-carson-county-roads-and-company-leadership/) treats Carson as primary. A parcel-level CAD confirmation was not obtained (Carson CAD's own portal is not reachable via automated search/fetch tools available this session — see Practical Notes below).

## Physical commitment
**Evidence found — strong.** TCEQ air permit application (Docket 2025-1898-AIR, applicant "Fermi Equipment Holdco, LLC") received Aug 1, 2025, administratively complete Aug 5, 2025; covers 90 combined-cycle + 3 flex-mode turbines, 6 emergency generator engines, 2 black-start engines, 2 fire pump engines. Public meeting held Dec 4, 2025 in Panhandle, TX. Final approval — described as the nation's second-largest Clean Air permit — granted ~Feb 25, 2026. A second application for an incremental 5 GW was filed March 27, 2026 (would bring total to ~11 GW).
- Sources: [TCEQ docket PDF](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/downloads/agency/decisions/agendas/backup/2025/2025-1898-air-appr.pdf), [NewsChannel10, 2026-02-25](https://www.newschannel10.com/2026/02/25/fermi-america-receives-nations-second-largest-clean-air-permit-project-matador/), [Amarillo Tribune, 2025-12-05](https://amarillotribune.org/2025/12/05/texas-commission-on-environmental-quality-and-fermi-america-hear-questions-and-comments-at-public-meeting-for-air-quality-per/) — captured 2026-08-05.
- **Contested:** a short seller (Fuzzy Panda) has alleged the air permit faces legal jeopardy via a lawsuit challenging TCEQ's approval process. Not independently verified this session — logged as an allegation, not a confirmed fact. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/fermi-america-stock-falls-after-short-seller-questions-permit-93CH-4669245) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — mixed/negative.** Fermi is a public company (FRMI, IPO'd on or around Oct 1, 2025). Financing activity is real but unstable: a $150M tenant grid-funding pact was **cancelled**, stock down ~65-80% since IPO, a $375M convertible notes offering priced July 2026 (dilutive, stock fell on announcement), and the company states in its own SEC filings it has **no binding tenant contract**.
- Sources: [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4853459-fermi-execution-fears-mount-after-terminated-deal-upgrade), [24/7 Wall St, 2026-07-10](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/10/fermi-america-sinks-11-5-with-375-million-offering/), [Yahoo Finance — tenant cancellation](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/fermi-frmi-down-33-4-030807469.html) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Incentive filings
**No JETI match — confirmed and structural, not a dead end.** Checked directly against the Texas Comptroller's current JETI agreements list (9 active agreements, none in tech/data-center sector, none named Fermi). This is expected, not incidental: the JETI Act's NAICS-code eligibility list statutorily excludes data centers. No data center can hold a JETI agreement, by design. **However, on manual re-check of the raw Comptroller §151.359 "Qualifying Data Center" registry (a separate sales-tax-exemption program, not JETI), a direct match was found: "Fermi Data Center 1," effective 2025-09-05, owners listed as Fermi Inc., Fermi Equipment Holdco LLC, Firebird Equipment Holdco LLC, Fermi Campus 1 LLC, Fermi Warehouse LLC, Fermi High Voltage Warehouse LLC, Fermi Turbine Warehouse LLC, Fermi Mobile Gen LLC, and Fermi Construction LLC** — an exact-name match to the TCEQ permit applicant. This scores under the registry-match cap (7/15), not the higher JETI/abatement tier. Separately, Fermi's own gas-generation plant (the TCEQ-permitted facility under Physical Commitment above) is a dispatchable electric-generation facility and would independently need to be evaluated for JETI eligibility on its own terms — that's a distinct question from the data center's own ineligibility, not addressed further here.
- Sources: [Comptroller JETI current agreements](https://comptroller.texas.gov/economy/development/prop-tax/jeti/current-agreements.php), [Comptroller Data Center lists](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/data-centers/data-center-lists.php) (manually re-verified as raw HTML 2026-08-05), [Ryan LLP — JETI NAICS eligibility](https://ryan.com/about-ryan/news-and-insights/2025/texas-jeti-incentive-strategy/) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — negative/high-risk.** Fermi America is a development-stage company (launched June 2025) with **no prior operating history, no constructed facilities, and no binding tenant contracts**, per its own SEC risk disclosures. Co-founded by Rick Perry (former TX Governor/US Energy Secretary — political/policy background, not a prior data-center or power-plant delivery record) and Toby Neugebauer. A pending class-action lawsuit alleges the company overstated tenant demand. The proposed nuclear cooling design is first-of-its-kind and unlicensed (IEEFA concern), adding execution risk beyond typical site delivery.
- Sources: [E&E News](https://www.eenews.net/articles/rick-perry-led-company-eyes-doe-loan-for-massive-ai-project/), [SEC Form S-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2071778/000121390025085175/ea0252333-01.htm), [GreentechLead — IEEFA](https://greentechlead.com/energy-news/fermi-americas-4456-mw-ai-nuclear-project-faces-rising-costs-and-regulatory-risks-says-ieefa-54168) — captured 2026-08-05.
