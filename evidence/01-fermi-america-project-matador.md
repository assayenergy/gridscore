## Incentive filings
**Correction to the prior score, not new news — this fact existed in October 2025 and was missed in the original research pass.**

The prior capture scored this at 7/15 based only on the Comptroller's §151.359 "Qualifying Data Center" sales-tax registry match. On this refresh, I obtained and read (via OCR, since it's a scanned document) the actual **Tax Abatement Agreement between Carson County, Texas and FERMI, INC.**, filed with the Carson County District Clerk on **2025-10-27**, following a Commissioners Court public hearing on **2025-08-18** and Commissioners Court approval on **2025-10-27**. Key terms, read directly from the document: governed by **Chapter 312 of the Texas Tax Code**; creates/uses **Carson County Reinvestment Zone 12**; grants a **100% exemption from ad valorem taxes** on the incremental value of Eligible Property in each "Phase Abatement Area" (base year 2025; pre-existing value stays fully taxable) for up to the statutory maximum of **10 years per phase**; structured for **up to 15 phases**, each phase covering a data center plus its associated power generation; abatement timing is tied to each phase's own Commercial Operations Date; includes a Payments-in-Lieu-of-Taxes provision (Section IV(D), terms not fully reviewed this pass).

This is exactly the "**executed** county/municipal tax abatement agreement" category the rubric scores up to the full 15 points — a materially different, stronger evidentiary category than the §151.359 registry match, which remains true and additive but is capped lower on its own.
