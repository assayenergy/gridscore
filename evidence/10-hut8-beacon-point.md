# Evidence Log — Beacon Point (Hut 8)

County: Nueces · Announced: 1,000 MW · Sponsor: Hut 8 Corp.

## Site control
**Evidence found — partial/self-reported.** 525-acre site near Corpus Christi, per Hut 8's own site page. No independent CAD/deed confirmation found (dead end).
- Source: [Hut 8 site page](https://www.hut8.com/data-centers/beacon-point) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Physical commitment
**Evidence found — partial, unusual claim.** Hut 8 states backup generation is on-site. The company also claims the site sits **outside Robstown city limits and therefore outside city/county permitting jurisdiction** for this project — an unusual, self-reported regulatory-gap claim, not independently verified. If accurate, TCEQ air permitting would be the primary applicable process, but no docket number was found for it this session (dead end).
- Source: [KRIS TV](https://www.kristv.com/news/local-news/in-your-neighborhood/corpus-christi/calallen/beacon-point-ai-massive-data-center-planned-for-nueces-county) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Financial commitment
**Evidence found — strong.** 1,000MW utility interconnection agreement with AEP Texas (named counterparty), disclosed via SEC 8-K. Two 15-year IT leases (352MW + 352MW = 704MW) with the same unnamed "investment-grade" tenant bring total base-term contract value to $19.6B; first two phases represent ~$17B combined capital investment. Strong SEC-filed financial evidence, though the actual tenant remains unnamed — flag that as an open question (can't verify tenant creditworthiness independently).
- Sources: [PRNewswire, 2026-07-20](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hut-8-fully-commercializes-1-gw-beacon-point-ai-data-center-campus-with-second-352-mw-it-lease-bringing-campus-level-base-term-contract-value-to-19-6-billion-302829514.html), [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1964789/000110465926084862/tm2620835d1_ex99-1.htm) — captured 2026-08-05.

## Incentive filings
**No public evidence found (as of 2026-08-05).** Not found on the JETI current-agreements list or the Comptroller's Qualifying Data Center registry — dead end on both.

## Sponsor track record
**Evidence found — positive, short history.** Hut 8 delivered Beacon Point's utility interconnection and fully commercialized the 1GW campus within its own announced timeline (2025–2026). Hut 8 itself is a relatively young company (2018-founded, formerly a pure Bitcoin miner) now pivoting into AI infrastructure — track record is positive but short, with no multi-cycle delivery history yet to draw on.
