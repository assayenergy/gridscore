> ## Market-opacity finding (carry into the writeup)
> **There is no public mechanism to reconcile a named, announced project against ERCOT's own
> interconnection queue.** ERCOT publishes aggregate MW totals for the large-load queue only
> (~226 GW as of Nov 2025, growing to ~474.7 GW by June 2026, ~90% attributed to data centers) —
> not a project directory, not a county-by-county named list, not anything that lets an outside
> observer match "Project Matador" or "Frontier" to a specific queue entry. The only per-project
> confirmation available publicly is when a sponsor itself discloses ERCOT approval in its own
> press release (e.g., Riot's Corsicana 1GW, PowerHouse's 500MW tranche) — i.e., the public has
> to take the sponsor's word for its own interconnection status; there's no independent registry
> to check it against. This is itself a data point for the "scoring what's real" piece: the queue
> that regulators and press cite as evidence of a boom is, by design, anonymous. The PUC's
> transparency rulemaking (due by Dec 2026 per SB6) is aimed at closing exactly this gap.

This means "cross-checked against ERCOT's public county-level large-load aggregates" can only be
claimed in the qualitative sense (queue-growth context, SB6's 75MW+ large-load threshold, ERCOT's
own load-category split) — not as a matched, line-by-line reconciliation. No further work will
close that gap; it's a documented limitation, not a to-do.

## Decisions locked with Vish (2026-08-05)

1. **Fermi America (Carson County, #1, 11GW)** — kept as-is despite the scale gap over #2. Decision: score evidence, not vibes — the size disparity is itself a data point the rubric will surface, not a reason to pre-filter. The Carson/Potter/Randall county ambiguity carries into Session 2 as a Site Control research question, not a listing problem.
2. **Poolside Project Horizon (Pecos County, #3)** — kept as originally announced (2GW) despite the since-deteriorated status (lost anchor tenant, scaled back). Same reasoning as above; the deterioration itself is exactly the kind of evidence Session 2 should capture and date-stamp, not a reason to exclude the project from the universe.
3. **Bitcoin-mining-to-HPC pivots (Marathon #19, Cipher #17, Riot #12)** — kept. Consistent with ERCOT's own queue categorization treating data centers and digital-asset mining as adjacent load categories, and with the "evidence not vibes" principle in both directions — origin story isn't a scoring input, evidence is.
4. **Google Panhandle sites** — split into two rows (#13 Armstrong County, #14 Haskell County); third unnamed site dropped from the universe (see note above). List is now 21 projects.
5. **ERCOT county-level cross-check** — see the market-opacity finding at the top of this file. Documented as a permanent limitation, not a pending task.
6. **TBD announcement dates** — left as-is; will be tightened opportunistically as Session 2's source-diving naturally surfaces primary press releases, not as a dedicated pass.
